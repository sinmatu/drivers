Company

Sin Matu Enterprise Sdn. Bhd.

Established in 1997, Sin Matu Enterprise Sdn. Bhd. engages in the wholesale distribution of construction materials as well as import and export operations, providing logistical and supply-chain support to the wider Sin Matu Group.

The company strengthened its operational capabilities through its main operational hub at reclaimed land off Jalan Patau-Patau, Labuan, commencing operations there in 2005.

Its in-house fleet includes transportation and construction equipment such as lorries, shovels and excavators, supporting efficient material handling, logistics and site operations.

Lorry System

Built around daily transport operations.

The Lorry System provides a focused workspace for recording work performed by the fleet, reviewing submitted hours, and reconciling operational records and amounts.

It keeps day-to-day entries simple while giving staff and management a consistent record for reporting and review.

What the system covers

01Work entries by lorry, date, start time and end time
02Normal, overtime, lunch OT and night-shift calculations
03Public-holiday handling and automatic shift numbering
04Records, review workflow, reconciliation and reports
05Per-lorry totals, subtotals and consolidated grand totals
