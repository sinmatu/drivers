About Us

Built to support the movement of materials and operations.

Sin Matu Enterprise Sdn. Bhd. was established in 1997. The company engages in the wholesale distribution of construction materials as well as import and export operations.

As part of its continuing operational development, the company commenced operations at its main hub on reclaimed land off Jalan Patau-Patau, Labuan, in 2005.

With its own transportation and construction equipment, Sin Matu Enterprise is able to support material handling, logistics and site operations efficiently.
