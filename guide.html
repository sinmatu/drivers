Lorry System · Work Hours & Reports

User Guide

This guide explains the normal working flow of the Lorry System: select the active lorry, enter work hours, review records, correct or check entries, then prepare monthly reports and exports.

Normal Workflow

Use the system in this order

The system is designed to keep data entry simple while preserving enough detail for checking and reporting.
Sign in with the assigned account Select the active lorry and enter work hours Check submitted work in Records Review, correct or mark entries checked Prepare monthly lorry reports Export Excel or PDF when required
Core rule: the system records work against the selected lorry. Always confirm the Active Lorry before submitting entries.
01 · Roles

Manager and Staff

There are only two supported user roles. There is no separate Driver login in the current system.

Staff

Staff can enter work, open Records, use the Review Queue, view Reports and see calculated amounts in Review and Reports.

Manager

Manager has the same operational access plus Manager-only controls such as Settings and Void Entry.

Simple access model: Staff handles daily operations. Manager maintains the system rules and has the additional authority needed for administration.

6. SubmitThe system validates overlaps and assigns shifts.

Automatic Shift Number

The first work period for a lorry on a date becomes Shift 1. The next non-overlapping work period becomes Shift 2, then Shift 3, and so on.

Overlap Protection

The system checks new rows against each other and against existing active work entries for the same lorry. Overlapping work should be corrected before submission.

Overnight entry: if End is earlier than Start, the system treats the End time as the next calendar day. In Records and Reports, the actual end date is shown so the overnight period is clear.
03 · Records

Records are the operational work history

Records intentionally focus on work information rather than money.
FieldPurpose
DateWork date, sortable newest or oldest first.
LorryThe lorry the work belongs to.
ShiftAutomatically assigned Shift 1, Shift 2, and so on.
Start / EndActual work period. Overnight End shows the actual next date.

No amount column in Records. This keeps Records focused on work history. Amounts belong in Review and Reports.
04 · Review Queue

Check and correct submitted work

Review is placed inside Records under the Review Queue tab.

Mark Checked

Use when the submitted entry is correct and no changes are required.

Correction Required

Use when the entry needs to be corrected before it is considered final.

Correct Entry

Update the lorry, Start, End, Public Holiday entitlement and correction reason. The original submission is retained for reference.

Manager-only: Void Entry excludes an incorrect record from normal Records, Reports and overlap checking while preserving the audit record in Firestore.
05 · Hour Calculation

Normal, OT and Night Shift

All payable work is grouped into three main categories.

Normal

Hours within the configured normal working window that do not fall into a higher-priority category.

OT

OT includes after-hours work, Sunday/off-day work, Public Holiday entitlement and lunch overtime under the configured rules.

Night Shift

Hours inside the configured night window. Night Shift has priority over OT and Normal.

PriorityNight Shift → OT → Normal.
Decimal hoursHours are displayed as decimals. Example: 2 hours 30 minutes = 2.50 hours; 1 hour 59 minutes = 1.98 hours after rounding.
Sunday / Off DayCounted under OT.
Public HolidayWhen PH entitlement is ticked and the date matches a configured Public Holiday, eligible hours are counted under OT unless Night Shift takes priority.
Lunch OTThe configured lunch window is treated as OT and included in the OT total.

07 · Manager Settings

Maintain the calculation rules

Settings is Manager-only because changes here affect future hour and amount calculations.

Working Hours

Normal start, Normal end, Saturday normal end, lunch start/end and Night Shift start/end.

Rates

Normal rate/hour, OT rate/hour and Night Shift rate/hour.

Lorries

Add and maintain the lorry list used by Work Entry, Records and Reports.

Public Holidays

Add Public Holiday dates and names so PH entitlement can be calculated correctly.

Before changing rates or time boundaries: confirm the business rule first. Settings directly affect calculated categories and amounts.
08 · Good Practice

Keep the records reliable

Most problems can be avoided by following a few simple habits.
Confirm the Active Lorry before key-in. Every row in the batch is submitted against that selected lorry.
Use the actual Start and End times. Do not round times unless the business process specifically requires it.
Tick PH entitlement correctly. This affects the category calculation.
Use Review for corrections. Avoid creating a second overlapping entry to replace a wrong one.
Use Reports for final checking and export. Records is the operational history; Reports is the reporting document.
Do not delete history manually. Manager Void keeps the audit trail while excluding an invalid entry from normal calculations.
